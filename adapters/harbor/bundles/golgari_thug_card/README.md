# Golgari Thug card task bundle

This Harbor bundle implements RAV #87 Golgari Thug. The printed black `{1}{B}` 1/1 has Dredge 4 and a trigger when it is put into a graveyard from the battlefield. Its controller targets a creature card in their graveyard and puts that card on top of its owner's library. Current Oracle's “dies” wording preserves that event; Human Warrior is recorded as identity but is outside the current executable subtype model.

See `varieties/magic/tasks/rav-golgari-thug-pvp/README.md` and `source-note.md` for the complete task and source reconciliation. The diagnostic authority earns no card credits or reward.
