# Autochthon Wurm card task

This bundle evaluates the candidate-owned RAV Autochthon Wurm definition in a shuffled, full-game PvP duel. Both players start from legal 60-card RAV decks and ordinary opening hands. The pilot builds its creature board from its shuffled deck, then casts Autochthon Wurm with nine Convoke contributors and six mana from the pool.

The reference checks the exact `{10}{G}{G}{G}{W}{W}` cost and 9/14 characteristics, two green/two white/five generic Convoke contributions, live Convoke and Trample, two cast blockers in an attacker-submitted combat order, one lethal damage to each blocker, seven Trample damage, both lethal-damage state-based actions and graveyard moves, terminal win, deterministic replay, and three gameplay mutations.
