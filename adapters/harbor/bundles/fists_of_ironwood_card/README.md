# Fists of Ironwood card task

This bundle evaluates RAV Fists of Ironwood in a seeded full-game PvP duel. The Aura must attach to the exact Watchwolf, grant it Trample, and resolve its mandatory ETB trigger to create two green 1/1 Saprolings. The Watchwolf attacks and is blocked; the trace checks lethal damage to the blocker and the exact two excess Trample damage before the match continues to a terminal result.

The authority requires deterministic replay and detects removed-trigger, removed-Trample, and wrong-token-count mutations. Passing qualifies one pilot card only and grants no credit across the RAV 291-name/306-printing denominator or Harbor reward.
