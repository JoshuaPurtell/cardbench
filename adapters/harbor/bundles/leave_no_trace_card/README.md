# Leave No Trace card task

This bundle evaluates the candidate-owned RAV Leave No Trace definition in a shuffled, full-game PvP duel. The fixture begins with an opposing white enchantment, a second white enchantment controlled by the pilot, and an opposing blue enchantment. Leave No Trace must destroy both white enchantments through Radiance and preserve the blue one.

The authority validates the `{1}{W}` payment, exact target and destruction source, both graveyard moves, preserved off-color permanent, ordered resolution, terminal result, deterministic replay, and three gameplay mutations. This diagnostic task awards zero card credit and zero Harbor reward; independent review and a candidate trial remain pending.
