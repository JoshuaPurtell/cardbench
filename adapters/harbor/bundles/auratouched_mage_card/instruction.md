# CardBench Magic single-card task: Auratouched Mage

Implement the RAV Auratouched Mage card in `varieties/magic/sets/ravnica_city_of_guilds/src/cards/auratouched_mage.rs`. Provide a direct `CardDefinition` literal and candidate-owned ETB `TriggeredAbilityBinding`.

The trusted authority runs shuffled 60-card two-player games that select a compatible Aura and, separately, fail to find while compatible choices remain. Each game continues through legal combat to a terminal result. The authority checks the Mage's printed identity and characteristics, ordered cast and trigger receipts, private search and attachment events, deterministic replay, and compiled semantic mutations. The task reports one passed identity among 291 RAV names and 306 printings with zero card credit and zero reward.
