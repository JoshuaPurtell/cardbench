# CardBench Magic single-card task: Compulsive Research

Implement the RAV Compulsive Research definition in `varieties/magic/sets/ravnica_city_of_guilds/src/cards/compulsive_research.rs`.

The trusted authority runs shuffled 60-card two-player games. It verifies the exact {2}{U} spell, target-player semantics, three draws before the discard decision, recipient-only visibility and submission, the one-land and two-distinct-card choices, deterministic replay, gameplay mutations, and a terminal result. The diagnostic covers one identity in a denominator of 291 RAV names and 306 printings. It grants no card credit or reward.
