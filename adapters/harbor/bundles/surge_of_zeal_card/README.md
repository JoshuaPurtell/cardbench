# Surge of Zeal RAV card task

This bundle covers Surge of Zeal (RAV #146), a `{R}` instant whose Radiance effect gives haste to the target creature and other creatures sharing one of its colors until end of turn. The trusted local authority checks the exact payment, cross-controller recipient set, a colorless exclusion, and a same-turn attack by a newly cast target in a terminal shuffled PvP game.
