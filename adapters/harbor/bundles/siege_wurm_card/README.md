# Siege Wurm card task

This bundle evaluates the candidate-owned RAV Siege Wurm definition in a shuffled, full-game PvP duel. Both players start from legal 60-card RAV decks and ordinary opening hands. The pilot develops its board by casting RAV creatures and Scatter the Seeds, then casts Siege Wurm using seven untapped creatures for Convoke.

The reference checks Siege Wurm's exact `{5}{G}{G}` cost and 5/5 characteristics, two green and five generic Convoke contributions, live Convoke and Trample, a cast Watchwolf blocking the Wurm, 3 damage to the blocker, 2 trample damage to the opponent, the blocker state-based action and graveyard move, full-game terminal result, deterministic replay, and three gameplay mutations. It awards zero card credit and zero Harbor reward; independent review and a candidate trial remain pending.
