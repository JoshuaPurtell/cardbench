# Cleansing Beam card task

This bundle evaluates the candidate-owned RAV Cleansing Beam definition in a shuffled, full-game PvP duel. The fixture begins with an opposing white 2/2 creature, a second white 2/2 creature controlled by the pilot, and an opposing blue 2/2 creature. Cleansing Beam must deal 2 to both white creatures through Radiance, put them into their owners’ graveyards by state-based actions, and preserve the blue creature.

The authority validates the `{4}{R}` payment, exact target and damage amounts, both lethal-damage state-based actions and graveyard moves, preserved off-color permanent, spell resolution order, terminal result, deterministic replay, and three gameplay mutations. This diagnostic task awards zero card credit and zero Harbor reward; independent review and a candidate trial remain pending.
