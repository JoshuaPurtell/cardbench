# Dowsing Shaman card task

This bundle evaluates RAV Dowsing Shaman in a bounded, offline full-game duel. The authority puts Fists of Ironwood onto the exact Elf that dies in combat, then verifies Dowsing Shaman's `{2}{G}`, tap ability returns that exact Aura from its controller's graveyard to hand. The duel continues to a terminal result, with deterministic replay and semantic mutation checks.

The diagnostic covers one pilot identity only. It grants zero RAV card credit and zero Harbor reward.
