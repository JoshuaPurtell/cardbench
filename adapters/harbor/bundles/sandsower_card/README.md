# Sandsower card task

This bundle evaluates the candidate-owned RAV Sandsower definition and activated ability in a shuffled, full-game PvP duel. Both players use legal 60-card decks and ordinary opening hands. The pilot casts Sandsower and three other RAV creatures before paying the ability's three-creature tap cost.

The reference checks exact `{3}{W}` payment and 1/3 characteristics, three distinct controlled creatures tapped as costs, Sandsower remaining untapped, an untapped opposing Watchwolf target that becomes tapped only on ability resolution, ordered receipts, deterministic replay, a terminal pilot win, and four gameplay mutations. It awards zero card credit and zero Harbor reward; independent review and candidate trial remain pending.
