# Seed Spark card task

This bundle evaluates the candidate-owned RAV Seed Spark definition in a shuffled, full-game PvP duel. The opponent establishes a Boros Signet, and the pilot casts Seed Spark with the exact artifact as its target. The ordered trace proves that the Signet is destroyed and moved to the graveyard before two distinct green 1/1 Saproling creature tokens are created, then follows those tokens into combat through the terminal result.

The authority also validates the card identity, deterministic replay, exact payment and event order, and five semantic gameplay mutations. This diagnostic task awards zero card credit and zero Harbor reward; an independent review and candidate trial remain pending.
