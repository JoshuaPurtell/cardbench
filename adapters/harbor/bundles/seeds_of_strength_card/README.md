# Seeds of Strength card task

This bundle evaluates RAV Seeds of Strength in a shuffled, full-game PvP duel. The authority casts the green-white `{G}{W}` Instant on one Watchwolf and two Elves of Deep Shadow. It checks the exact spell and creature objects, all three independently created +1/+1 effects, temporary characteristics and their cleanup reversion, then verifies that the same creatures attack and deal their restored power as combat damage.

The match continues to a terminal result and replays deterministically. Gameplay mutations change the P/T modifier, remove the third modifier, or alter the printed cost. Passing qualifies only this single-card pilot and awards no credit among the RAV 291-name/306-printing denominator or Harbor reward.
