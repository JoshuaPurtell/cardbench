# Oathsworn Giant card task

This bundle evaluates RAV Oathsworn Giant in a legal seeded 60-card PvP game. The authority checks its `{4}{W}{W}` cast and printed 3/4 vigilance, then casts Watchwolf for `{G}{W}` first. When the Giant resolves, Watchwolf must be 3/5 with vigilance and the Giant itself must remain 3/4. Both attack, deal damage, and remain untapped after combat; the full game must replay deterministically.

Three live-game semantic mutants cover the +0/+2 bonus, granted vigilance, and printed vigilance. Passing qualifies only this pilot and awards no credit across the 291-card RAV denominator or Harbor reward.
