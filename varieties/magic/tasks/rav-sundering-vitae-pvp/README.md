# Sundering Vitae card task

Implement RAV Sundering Vitae as a green `{2}{G}` Instant with Convoke and the artifact-or-enchantment destruction effect in the candidate-owned card module. Preserve exact RAV #185 identity and target legality.

The trusted local authority builds two shuffled 60-card decks, casts Sundering Vitae at an opposing Boros Signet, pays the green symbol by tapping a green creature for Convoke and the generic portion with two white mana, verifies the exact destruction and graveyard receipts, then continues through combat to a terminal seat-zero win. It replays the fixed seed and checks mutations to Convoke, cost, and destruction behavior.

This is a local diagnostic task. It does not award cards or reward, and its reference qualification does not run Harbor or provider trials.
