# Grave-Shell Scarab source note

RAV #211 is Grave-Shell Scarab, a black-green `{2}{B}{G}{G}` 4/4 creature. Its printed abilities are Dredge 1 and `{1}, Sacrifice Grave-Shell Scarab: Draw a card.` The source record identifies the Ravnica: City of Guilds printing and supplies its Oracle text. The archived October 1, 2005 ruling confirms that the Scarab may pay for its own activated ability and then use its own Dredge ability to replace that draw.

The authority checks the printed mana cost, colors, creature type, power and toughness, Dredge number, activation cost, source sacrifice, and draw effect. It checks both legal outcomes of the ability's draw: selecting the sacrificed Scarab for Dredge 1, and declining Dredge so the ordinary draw occurs.
