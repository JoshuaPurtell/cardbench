# Goliath Spider single-card PvP task

This isolated task covers RAV #168 Goliath Spider and Reach in a legal
constructed match. A real opposing Skyknight Legionnaire must attack while
Goliath Spider is available to block. The authority checks green plus six
generic payment, live 7/6 Reach characteristics, the legal block, combat damage
that kills the flyer, continued play to a terminal game, deterministic replay,
a Reach-removal mutation, and compile-valid stub rejection.

This qualifies only the Goliath Spider pilot trace; it awards no whole-set
coverage or Harbor reward. Candidate source must remain a declarative card
definition without copied card rules prose, art, or flavor text.
