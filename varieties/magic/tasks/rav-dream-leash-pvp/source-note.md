# Source note: Dream Leash (RAV #45)

The RAV catalog records one printing, collector number 45, named Dream Leash, with definition ID `RAV-DREAM-LEASH`. The original printing is a blue `{3}{U}{U}` Enchantment — Aura. Its printed rules say to enchant a permanent, permit casting it only on a tapped permanent, and give its controller control of the enchanted permanent.

Public card record and original-text source: <https://mtg.wtf/card/rav/45/Dream-Leash>. The record identifies RAV #45, mana cost, card types, and its `originalText` alongside current wording. Cross-check: <https://mtgseeker.com/card/rav/45/dream-leash>.

Current Oracle wording frames the restriction as not choosing an untapped permanent as the spell's target while casting. Its October 1, 2005 ruling clarifies that this tapped condition is checked only when the Aura is cast; the permanent may untap before resolution and may remain untapped after attachment. This is a wording clarification, not a change to the RAV behavior.
