# Surveilling Sprite RAV card task

Implement Surveilling Sprite (RAV #67), a `{1}{U}` blue 1/1 creature with Flying and an optional dies trigger that draws its controller a card. In an ordinary shuffled two-player game, cast it with exact blue payment and attack past a live, able-to-block Lurking Informant. The opponent then casts Char for `{2}{R}` and deals four damage to the Sprite. Verify lethal damage, the state-based action, its move to the graveyard, and the optional trigger with its source already in the graveyard. Trace both accepting the draw and declining it, with the accepted draw moving a card into hand before ability resolution completes. A later combat ends in a seat-zero win. Mutations cover Flying, cost, power, trigger presence, draw effect, and optionality. Diagnostic reports do not credit RAV coverage or Harbor reward.
