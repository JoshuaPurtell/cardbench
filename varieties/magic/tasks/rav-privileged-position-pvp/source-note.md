# Privileged Position source note (RAV #251)

The RAV catalog identifies one printing of Privileged Position as collector number 251. The RAV printing is an Enchantment costing `{2}{G/W}{G/W}{G/W}`. Its printed ability is: “Other permanents you control can't be the targets of spells or abilities your opponents control.”

Public RAV card record and current Oracle text: <https://mtg.wtf/card/rav/251/Privileged-Position>. The RAV printed text and cost are also transcribed in the RAV product listing at <https://www.nobleknight.com/P/2147379155/Privileged-Position-R>. The catalog remains authoritative for set identity and the one-printing denominator.

Current Oracle rewrites the ability as “Other permanents you control have hexproof.” Hexproof preserves the printed opponent-only targeting restriction. Shroud is broader because it also prevents the permanent's controller from targeting it; this task does not treat Shroud as equivalent. The trace therefore checks both an opponent's rejected Putrefy cast and the controller's legal Gather Courage cast targeting the same protected creature.

The task follows `varieties/magic/tasks/rav-set-pvp/RAV_PVP_SOURCE_CONTRACT.md`: the RAV printing controls identity and semantics, and current Oracle is used only to reconcile terminology. No subtype model limitation applies because the printed card is an Enchantment without a subtype. No art, flavor, price, or set-context fact is used as a mechanic.
