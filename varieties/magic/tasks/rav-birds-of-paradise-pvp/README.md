# Birds of Paradise card task

Implement Birds of Paradise's candidate-owned card definition and mana-ability binding in `varieties/magic/sets/ravnica_city_of_guilds/src/cards/birds_of_paradise.rs`.

The authority plays a shuffled 60-card two-player game, casts Birds of Paradise for `{G}`, observes its flying 0/1 characteristics, taps it for white while paying Watchwolf's `{G}{W}` cost, and continues to a terminal combat win. It checks typed payment and mana-source events, deterministic replay, and five semantic mutations.

This is a diagnostic task for one of 291 unique RAV identities and 306 printings. It awards no card credit or reward. No provider trial is included.
