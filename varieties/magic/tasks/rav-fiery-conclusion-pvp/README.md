# Fiery Conclusion RAV card task

Implement the candidate-owned Fiery Conclusion module for RAV #122: a `{1}{R}` red instant that requires its caster to sacrifice a creature and deals 5 damage to a target creature. The card module also owns the typed additional-spell-cost binding.

The authority uses an ordinary shuffled 60-card two-player game. Seat one attacks with a Watchwolf; seat zero responds before blockers, pays red plus one generic mana, sacrifices its Elves of Deep Shadow, and deals 5 damage to the attacking Watchwolf. The cost receipt and immediate sacrifice zone change precede the cast receipt. The spell resolves, lethal damage causes the target's state-based action before combat damage, and seat zero eventually wins. Diagnostic reports credit no RAV coverage or Harbor reward.
