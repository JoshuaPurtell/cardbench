# Moroii RAV card task

Implement the literal RAV #216 Moroii definition and its upkeep trigger in the candidate-owned module. The creature is blue-black, costs `{2}{U}{B}`, is a 4/4 with Flying, and makes its controller lose 1 life at the beginning of that controller's upkeep.

The shuffled two-player 60-card duel checks the exact four-mana spell receipt, live 4/4 Flying characteristics, trigger source and incarnation, opponent-upkeep exclusion, and the single controller life-loss event. The flying Moroii must attack through the opponent's ground creature, and the full match must end in a pilot victory and replay identically. Cost, base-stat, Flying, and trigger-removal mutations are checked. The task awards no card credit or reward.
