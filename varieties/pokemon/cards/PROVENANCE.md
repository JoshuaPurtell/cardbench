# Provenance and publication boundary

The initial Dragon Frontiers and Holon Phantoms card curriculum was migrated
from `JoshuaPurtell/engine-bench-tcg` at commit
`c39c9ba8918a699dd7c58ac7932119d5289a9913`.

The repository-level MIT license covers CardBench-authored code. It does not
claim ownership of Pokémon names, rules text, set names, trademarks, or other
third-party game data. No card art, scans, or commercial client assets are
included.

Public task material consists of structured specifications, starting stubs,
and hashes. Reference implementations and evaluator tests are stored in a
trusted sealed namespace and are not copied into agent workspaces. Before
redistributing the structured card data outside this benchmark, confirm the
desired data/licensing policy.
